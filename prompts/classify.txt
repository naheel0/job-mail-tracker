  "company": "<company name or null>",
  "role": "<job title or null>"}}

label meanings:
- applied: confirmation that the user applied / application received
- recruiter: a recruiter or company reached out about a job
- interview: interview invite or scheduling
- rejected: application rejected / not selected
- offer: job offer
- followup: reminder to follow up on an application
- promo: platform alert / digest / recommendation / newsletter, not tied to a real application
- noise: everything else (unrelated mail)

Platform rules (Indeed, Naukri, LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Shine, TimesJobs, foundit):
- A job ALERT, digest, recommendation, or promo ("and 2 more new jobs",
  "jobs you might like", "top companies are hiring", "job search tips",
  "featured jobs for you") -> label "promo", NEVER "recruiter" or "applied".
- If the email confirms the USER'S OWN application ("Indeed Application: ...",
  "application received"), label "applied" and set company to the REAL EMPLOYER
  named in the subject or body (pattern: "Role at Company in City" -> company is
  that Company), NEVER the platform name. Only use the platform name as company
  if no employer is stated anywhere.

Extract company and role only when clearly present. Lowercase values. Null otherwise.
